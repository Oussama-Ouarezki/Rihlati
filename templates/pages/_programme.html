{% load programme %} {% comment %} The programme, read-only. Shared by the public offer page, the traveler's own trip page and the agency editor, so the three never drift apart — a buyer comparing programmes and a family reading theirs at 4am are looking at the same rows, and if they rendered differently one of them would be wrong. The road is drawn, not authored: `static/road.js` measures the pins and injects one curve through them. Every step below is real content and reads fine without the script — a pin's emoji is repeated in the kind pill beside it, so nothing is only visible in the drawing. Times are printed exactly as stored. `time_of_day` is a string, never a TimeField: 04:30 must read as 04:30 to a family in Alger and to the guide standing in Jeddah. Converting it is the bug, not the feature. {% endcomment %}
